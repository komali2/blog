In 2025, [Framework did some things that made people mad](https://sgued.fr/blog/framework-omarchy/). 

Pause right here - already people reading this that are in the know are getting ready to say, "just SOME things? They PROMOTED a NAZI! They DONATED to a FASCIST!"

I was threatened with physical harm several times by bigots during [Black Lives Matter](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aoLMTy5BKSo) protests that I personally organized, as well as at woman's rights ("pro-choice") protests. I've been in the game since 2008 and have become only more anarchist every year that's gone by. I've shut down casual transphobia, homophobia, and racism I've encountered in person by people that assumed that just because I'm a cishet white guy I'm "on their side," getting shouts and threats in return. I've put time in on civic hack projects, cleaned up beaches and parks, and engage in daily direct action. Hell, I've even read some theory.

Therefore, I assert that I can speak on this subject and deserve to be heard in good faith, and that cynical dismissal of what I'm writing here is doing a disservice (in a small way) to progressivism / leftism, because even if my analysis is way off base or wrong, it comes from someone who deeply cares about our (leftists) shared values and the propagation and implementation thereof. 

So to make it perfectly clear: I'm an anarchist, trans rights are human rights, black and brown lives matter, dissolve all borders, dissolve all states, dissolve capitalism and currency and private property, and obliterate fascism.

In this post I want to analyze the ideas of moral contagion and moral absolutism, how no matter what Framework does, it will never be forgiven by community of online leftists, and how this tendency of ours to engage in this sort of thing is contrary to our ideology, let alone not being very pragmatic.


## What Framework Did

1. Donated to date around 14,400€ to Hyprland (continuing in 2026)
2. Possibly sent a free laptop to the Omarchy project, meaning, to DHH (not ongoing)
3. Has a yearly booth at Rails World as well as engages in sponsorship, potentially up to 48000 (continuing in 2026)

This is bad because DHH is racist, and Hyprland is led by one of those "we don't need a CoC just grow thicker skin" guys that likes to throw the r-word around (but would "never say the n word" /eyeroll).

So, Framework was [big cancelled](https://community.frame.work/t/framework-supporting-far-right-racists/75986). 

Here's where my confusion begins. My confusions: 

1. Omarchy is just a dumb collection of dot files maintained by an asshole, yet [its popularity](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ljGPvgMPOn8) is bringing a ton of normies [into linux world](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DYnc-xwNeTg). Normies who have no idea who DHH is and almost definitely don't share his beliefs. Why do we throw that consideration away?
2. Framework does a lot of good. It donates [tens of thousands](https://frame.work/tw/en/blog/framework-sponsorships) of dollars every year to various distros, desktop environments, and hackathons like NixOS, Arch, KDE, and Debian. It's basically the only professional-grade, linux-compatible laptop that is provably highly repairable and committed to [right to repair](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2022/12/framework-noctua-and-other-brands-add-official-3d-models-to-printables/). Pretend the Omarchy stuff didn't happen: Is there a more ethical professional-grade laptop manufacturer existent today? (MNT: no, it's not a realistically good enough tool to enable me to sell my labor to capitalists) So, why is nobody considering weighing this good against the bad it's done?
3. Considering the options on the table: Dell provides servers to the IDF, HP contracts with ICE, and Apple's Tim Cook gifted Trump an [obscene trophy with a 24-karat gold base](https://www.theverge.com/news/737757/apple-president-donald-trump-ceo-tim-cook-glass-corning). Why is it acceptable to boycott Framework and then immediately turn to buy from a company that's done far more measurable and material harm to people?
4. On that note, what's all this hulabaloo about Framework when we all quietly pay AWS every month to host our apps despite the fact that it's the cloud infra provider for ICE and Amazon [forces its workers to work next to the corpse](https://www.newsnationnow.com/business/amazon-employees-forced-work-corpse/) of their recently deceased coworker. There are other hosting options! Repeat per all the other atrocious and awful things the companies we give money to do: why do we let them get away with it, but Framework for some reason commits far less serious crimes but gets the greater punishment?
5. Rails World is the only remaining Rails conference - where else is Framework supposed to go? In any case, DHH as chairman of the board of Rails World receives no compensation from the organization.
6. Why don't other FOSS organizations get heat for partnering with e.g. AWS? E.g. GNOME for [gushing about its partnership with AWS](https://blogs.gnome.org/foundation/2025/06/10/gnome-has-a-new-infrastructure-partner-welcome-aws/).


## What Other People Have Said

I've asked these questions to many people, in good faith, trying to understand. I was thinking about dropping a bunch of quotes here, but any time this subject is brought up, anyone mentioned gets doxxed and harassed, so I want to avoid that. The Framework forum thread mentioned above has a pretty good collection of the various viewpoints. 

The answers to my confusion range, but generally fall into a couple categories: 

1. Any sort of support of fascism or racism or naziism whatsoever is completely intolerable. 

2. Framework was supposed to be "one of us," and its actions represent a betrayal.

3. The other bad companies we deal with every day are expected to be bad.

4. Frameworks actions alienate a portion of the community and make people participating feel "unpleasant or unsafe."
